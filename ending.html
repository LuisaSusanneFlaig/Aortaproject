SCIENCE COMMUNICATION

Why visualize
blood flow?

Blood flow is invisible, but it plays an important role in what happens inside the aorta. To make these complex processes more accessible beyond a medical context, this scrollytelling application used narrative visualization to translate medical data and scientific concepts into a visual experience. This makes abstract processes such as changes in blood flow easier to understand, while making the information more engaging and personally relatable.

The flow shown here is a simplified model that is available via the Vascular Model Repository and was adapted for this application. It does not predict exactly how blood moves inside an individual person's aorta.
